9.4 Every environment. Every story now has a forced-colors and a right-to-left baseline, and is checked for reflow at 200% and 400% zoom (WCAG 1.4.10), for anything still moving under reduced motion, and for any `backdrop-filter` left under reduced transparency. Fixes they found. Forced colors: on, selected and filled states are redrawn with system colours (a switch looked the same on and off, a radio lost its dot, a slider its track, a pressed toggle and a highlighted menu item their fill), and buttons, floating panels and separators keep an edge. Motion: a centred `Dialog` (and `CommandPalette`) flew in from the corner by half its own size, in normal and reduced motion alike; the entrance and exit now add to the element's own `translate` (`animation-composition: add`), so it grows and rises in place and does not move under reduced motion. Zoom: `Pagination` wraps, and a chart's hover tooltip stays inside the plot instead of widening the page. React Native: the OS Bold Text setting (iOS) now steps the Nexub font files up a weight, live; every exported component is checked for text that scales with the OS font size and for reading order, and the light/dark switch is checked while mounted. The docs Accessibility page lists the results per environment.
