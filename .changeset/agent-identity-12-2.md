`AgentAvatar` (beta): an AI agent's avatar, so an agent never looks like a colleague. A rounded-square app tile with a small sparkle in the bottom inline-end corner, in the same sizes as `Avatar`. Takes `icon` (the app glyph) or `src`, `name`, `size` and an optional `category` colour; screen readers hear "Payhub agent, AI agent" (new `aiAgent` message).

`AgentBadge` (beta): a soft `Badge` with a leading sparkle for an agent's chat header ("AI on your own key", "Buttons only"). Takes `icon`, `tone`, `category` and `size`.
