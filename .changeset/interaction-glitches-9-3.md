9.3 Interaction glitches. A browser interaction suite now runs on every overlay (Dialog, AlertDialog, Drawer, Popover, DropdownMenu, Menubar, Select, DatePicker, DateTimePicker) in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox: keyboard, pointer and touch open it, focus goes in and comes back, rapid and double activation settle in one state, and a modal's scroll lock does not move the page. A state matrix pushes every story through long content (200 characters, emoji, Arabic), empty, disabled, loading and invalid. Fixes they found: `Drawer` now moves focus to its panel on open (it stayed on the trigger behind the modal; `autoFocus` still focuses the first control); Enter, Escape and Backspace that belong to an IME composition no longer save `InlineEdit`, commit a typed `DatePicker` date, remove a `MultiSelect` chip or pop a `CommandPalette` page; a long text label on `Button` and `Badge` ends in an ellipsis inside its container instead of running past it. Note: a string label on `Button` and `Badge` is now wrapped in a `<span>`, so `getByText(label)` returns that span, not the button or badge; use `getByRole` or `.closest()` for the element itself.
